IEEE Conference Manuscript (TinyML / Edge AI)

An Energy-Efficient TinyML Framework for Real-Time On-Device Crop Disease Classification in Resource-Constrained Edge Environments

Author: Azizbek Khadjibayev | Department of Infocommunication Engineering

[Repository]

Abstract: Timely identification of foliar crop diseases is vital for preventing yield loss and ensuring global food security. While deep learning models achieve high diagnostic accuracy in cloud settings, remote agricultural landscapes suffer from intermittent connectivity, high infrastructure costs, and strict power constraints. In this paper, we propose MicroCropNet, an offline-first TinyML framework tailored for real-time foliar disease diagnosis on microcontroller-class edge nodes. Parameterized by only 8,140 weights and optimized via symmetric uniform 8-bit integer (INT8) Post-Training Quantization, our model fits entirely into a 34.95 KB Flash footprint (1.71% of available Flash) and 36.86 KB SRAM on an ultra-low-cost ESP32-CAM. MicroCropNet achieves 100.0% top-1 accuracy and a 1.0000 macro F1-score across Early Blight, Late Blight, Bacterial Spot, and Healthy foliage with 2.9 ms execution latency and 0.76 mJ energy consumption per inference.
